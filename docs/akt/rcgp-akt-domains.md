# Mapping to RCGP / AKT Feedback Domains

Each question should implicitly or explicitly map to **one or more** AKT domains. This keeps content **defensible** and **exam-aligned** with RCGP feedback trends.

Use these when:

- **Tagging or categorising** questions (e.g. our `topic` field or future `domains` metadata)
- **Generating** new questions (Prompt A) so coverage is balanced
- **Auditing** (Prompt B) to check alignment

---

## Clinical Domains

| Domain | Notes |
|--------|--------|
| **Cardiovascular** | IHD, HF, AF, hypertension, lipids, ECG interpretation |
| **Respiratory** | Asthma, COPD, pneumonia, ILD, breathlessness |
| **Dermatology** | Eczema, psoriasis, skin cancer, rashes, topical prescribing |
| **ENT / Ophthalmology** | Hearing, red eye, ENT emergencies, vertigo |
| **Mental health** | Depression, anxiety, psychosis, safeguarding, capacity |
| **Older adults / frailty** | Polypharmacy, falls, dementia, end-of-life |
| **Cancer & palliative care** | Red flags, referral, SACT, palliative prescribing |
| **Women's / men's health** | Contraception, menopause, pregnancy, sexual health |
| **Infectious disease** | Antimicrobial stewardship, sepsis, HIV, TB, travel |

---

## Cross-Cutting AKT Skills

| Skill | Examples |
|--------|--------|
| **Prescribing safety & monitoring** | DMARDs, lithium, methotrexate, monitoring intervals |
| **Antimicrobial stewardship** | When to prescribe, choice, duration, resistance |
| **Risk stratification** | Who to refer, when to investigate, safety-netting |
| **Evidence-based practice** | NNT, sensitivity/specificity, PPV, confidence intervals |
| **Data interpretation** | Graphs, lab results, screening stats, study results |
| **Referral thresholds** | 2WW, routine, urgent, when to escalate |
| **Equality & population health** | Health inequalities, screening eligibility |
| **Urgent vs routine care** | Red flags, same-day vs follow-up, when to admit |

---

## Using with Our Question Bank

- Our **topic** field often maps to one of the clinical domains (e.g. Cardiovascular, Respiratory, Mental Health) or to cross-cutting areas (e.g. Evidence-Based Practice, Prescribing).
- When adding new questions, choose a **topic** that fits one or more of the domains above so coverage stays balanced and aligned with RCGP feedback.
